## Flipkart case challenge

Case on a sustainable human settlement within 20 years on Mars. It weighs habitability, radiation, cost, and governance, then moves from Earth simulation to a permanent colony run by an OmniCore AI for life support, habitat, logistics, and health. Funding is about $3 billion, with success measured by return, efficiency, and long-term sustainability.

## Product ideation: Data Quality Index

Product case on making real-world pharmaceutical and provider data usable. Clinical, claims, and genomic sources are hard to combine because quality, format, and privacy rules differ. The proposal is a Data Quality Index that scores reliability, plus a centralized repository of cleaned, standardized data for providers and drug companies. Success is tracked by retrieval time, quality issues resolved, compliance, and how fast decisions get made.
