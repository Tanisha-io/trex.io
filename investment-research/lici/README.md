# LIC valuation (NSE: LICI)

Equity note, 6 November 2024. Price used: INR 946. View: hold.

### About Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC)

Established on 1 September 1956, the Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) is India's largest life insurance company, formed following the nationalisation of the life insurance industry and the consolidation of 245 insurers and provident societies. Wholly owned by the Government of India until its partial disinvestment in May 2022, LIC operates across individual and group life insurance, pension and annuity products, savings-linked policies and investment services.

With an extensive nationwide distribution network comprising individual agents, branches, bancassurance partners and digital channels, LIC has historically played a significant role in mobilising household savings and providing long-term financial protection. Its business model combines premium collection with long-term investment management, while its profitability is influenced by product mix, persistency, investment returns, claims experience and surplus distribution.

Following the entry of private insurers in 2000, LIC has operated in a competitive insurance market while retaining a substantial presence in the Indian life insurance sector. Its listing on the NSE and BSE in May 2022 marked its transition to a publicly traded company, bringing greater focus on shareholder returns, embedded value and the profitability of new business.

### Valuation

That last shift is why LIC is not valued like an industrial company. Premiums are not sales in the usual sense: a large share of surplus belongs to policyholders, the balance sheet is the investment book, and profit moves with markets, reserving, persistency and claims rather than with operating volume alone. A price-to-earnings or free-cash-flow multiple therefore says little on its own.

The measures that follow from the listing are embedded value, value of new business and its margin, and solvency. This note uses those, then a discounted-cash-flow range (WACC 18.05%, terminal growth 2.5%) that puts intrinsic value at about INR 850–950 a share, close to the price at the time. The view is a hold.
