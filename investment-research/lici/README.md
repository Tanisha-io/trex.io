# LIC (NSE: LICI)

Equity note on Life Insurance Corporation of India, 6 November 2024. Price used: INR 946. View: hold.

LIC is here because it is a life insurer, and that changes the valuation. Premiums are not sales in the industrial sense: much of the surplus belongs to policyholders, the balance sheet is dominated by the investment book, and profit can move with markets and reserving rather than with operating volume. A price-to-earnings or free-cash-flow multiple therefore says little on its own. The useful measures are embedded value, value of new business, and solvency. This note uses those, then a discounted-cash-flow range (WACC 18.05%, terminal growth 2.5%) that puts intrinsic value at about INR 850–950 a share, close to the price at the time.
